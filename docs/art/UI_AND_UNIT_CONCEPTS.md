## Direction

- UI: slate and brass panels, clear team accents, gold-only economy, and the current nine-command hero card. The battlefield and displayed statistics are illustrative, not an implementation or balance specification.
- Units: Worker, Shieldguard, Crossbow, and Wild Pact Stalker. Distinct tools and silhouettes, flat material colours, mitten hands and simple bevelled blocks or low-sided prisms.
- Views: front three-quarter, elevated gameplay camera, and overhead study. These are illustrative perspectives rather than calibrated orthographic blueprints; future Blender work must verify topology, equipment continuity and readability at actual sprite size.
- The suggested 300–700 triangles per unit is a modeling target, not a measured property of the artwork. Simplify bevels and shoulder pieces as needed.
- Team patches should be recolourable independently of faction materials.

## UI prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create polished concept art for LoveRTS, a top-down fantasy RTS, as a wide 16:9 full gameplay screen, not a website and not a poster. Functional restrained fantasy interface, dark slate inset panels, thin warm brass edges, muted ivory typography, blue player accents; flat simple icon illustrations, minimal ornament and generous readable spacing. Battlefield takes the upper 80 percent, with a thin top status strip and a compact bottom dock. Low-poly terrain, a few blocky human troops with blue cloth, trees, headquarters and a gold extractor in the battlefield, fixed elevated orthographic camera. No photorealism. Selected Warden hero and a few allies with clear rings. Top strip shows only gold 650, food 18/80, idle workers 2, timer 03:24, menu. Gold is the only resource, no lumber. Bottom left square readable minimap with fog; beside it hero portrait, HP and mana bars, small unit-group tiles and selected unit stats. Bottom right exactly a 3 by 3 grid of nine generously readable command buttons, each with a simple bold role icon, a short label, and a small hotkey. Exact row order: Move M, Bulwark W, Challenge E / Attack A, Stop S, Hold H / Patrol P, Stance Z, Upgrades U. Bulwark and Challenge show small 60 and 45 mana costs. Use a small red cost accent on unavailable Challenge to demonstrate a disabled state. Strong hierarchy, legible near-final game UI aesthetic, attractive but feasible with simple panel rectangles and reusable icons in LOVE. Tiny decorative rivets at most; no elaborate carved frame, no inventory, no extra resource bars, no overlapping text, no floating concept annotations. Clear readable battlefield behind a compact practical UI.

## Unit sheet prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a professional 3D low-poly game character MODELING REFERENCE SHEET for LoveRTS. Landscape sheet on warm light grey, four equal vertical columns with ample clean space, consistent scale and matched orientation across rows. Exactly four basic units, headings WORKER, SHIELDGUARD, CROSSBOW, STALKER. Each column has three clearly separated views of the SAME design with the SAME equipment: large front three-quarter neutral standing view at top; elevated orthographic gameplay view looking down at 60 degrees in middle; true orthographic overhead view at bottom. Small row labels FRONT / GAME CAMERA / TOP. The overhead figures must genuinely show the tops of heads, shoulders, weapons and feet, not another frontal figure. A simple dark silhouette thumbnail next to each overhead view demonstrates role readability.
Art direction: extremely simple charming chunky low-poly Blender primitive construction, visibly planar flat-shaded surfaces, no outlines, no painterly detail, no texture maps or sculpted surface detail. Roughly 300-700 triangle design intent per character, not a certified count. Large head, broad shoulders, short sturdy legs, mitten hands, huge readable equipment held apart from torso; clear gaps between arms and body. Each limb a single tapered four- or six-sided prism; torso bevelled block; heads simple 8-sided forms; minimal facial marks. Big unbroken flat material color regions, neutral matte lighting, subtle contact shadows.
WORKER: short tan tunic, blue hood cap and shoulder cloth, brown boots, one broad blocky construction mallet in right hand, small square tool pack, rounded compact outline. No mining pick and no lumber axe: this worker constructs buildings.
SHIELDGUARD: broadest humanoid shoulders, flat steel helmet with short blue crest, blue tabard, a huge simple rectangular shield on left side and short broad sword held on right. Shield should occupy a large distinct portion of the overhead silhouette, modest grey armour made of simple blocks, no filigree.
CROSSBOW: slimmer figure, dark leather vest and blue shoulder cape shaped as one stiff trapezoid, simple helmet, oversized crossbow held forward horizontally with a very broad T shape, one thick bolt, block limbs, no fingers or mechanism details. Weapon recognisable above.
STALKER: Wild Pact fast melee unit, lean slightly crouched figure in rust-brown leather and moss-green cloth with a large ochre team-colour shoulder patch, angular triangular wolf-eared hood rather than detailed fur, two short broad triangular blades projecting to each side. Head is a simple geometric hood shape, no fur grooming, no tiny accessories.
Keep equipment geometry easy to reproduce with cubes, bevelled cubes, low-sided cylinders and wedge meshes. No flowing hair, intricate anatomy, loose chains, individual fingers, tiny buckles, complex cloth folds, filigree, realistic metal wear or glossy PBR. Role should remain understandable as a small 48-pixel-high RTS unit. This is production-minded visual concept art, not elaborate fantasy illustration.
