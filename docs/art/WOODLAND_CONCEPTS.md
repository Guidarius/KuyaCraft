## Direction and production notes

Warm woodland adventure with original anthropomorphic animal characters. Rounded honey-oak frames, cream parchment panels, sage accents and chunky command tiles replace the previous slate-and-brass treatment.

Suggested visual role mappings:
- Worker: Mouse Builder — big round ears and construction mallet.
- Shieldguard: Hedgehog Guard — broad quill mass and shield.
- Crossbow: Squirrel Crossbow — T-shaped weapon and curled tail.
- Warden: Badger Warden — wide body, facial stripe and round shield.

These mappings are concept proposals, not committed faction lore or mechanical changes. Use the unit sheet for the intended modeling simplicity. The UI scene is more detailed and is a mood reference; simplify its foliage, fur, quills and architecture for production.

Build characters from low-sided volumes and bevelled blocks, with flat material regions and recolourable team patches. Quills should be a few large lobes; the squirrel tail should be a few thick connected volumes. Facial stripes should be material regions. Avoid individual hairs, quills and fingers. The 500–1000 triangle prompt target is unverified; actual topology and sprite-size readability require a Blender prototype.

The image views are illustrative, not calibrated orthographic blueprints. Portraits, names, counts, prices and statistics in the UI are placeholders; generated extra slogans are not proposed game copy. In particular, permanent upgrades still use the current XP rules, not the illustrated gold price. Preserve the live command labels, hotkeys and costs during any implementation.

## Unit generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a landscape production-minded modeling concept sheet for an original cozy medieval woodland RTS with anthropomorphic animals, evoking the woodland adventure warmth of Redwall without using its characters. Softer and CHUNKIER than standard low-poly fantasy. Warm cream background, clean spacious grid of four equal columns and three rows, understated small brown headings. Four upright bipedal units with clear roles: MOUSE BUILDER, HEDGEHOG GUARD, SQUIRREL CROSSBOW, BADGER WARDEN. All four are animal people, no human faces, no quadruped poses. Each column shows the SAME equipment and design in a large front three-quarter view, then an elevated orthographic RTS gameplay view looking down steeply, then a small true overhead study alongside a solid silhouette thumbnail. Exact labels for rows FRONT, GAME VIEW, TOP. Consistent design and size across views; badger is about 1.35 times the mouse body height, broad and sturdy.
Style: deliberately simple low-poly Blender-friendly construction with softened bevels, big heads, short thick limbs, rounded mitten paws, two large blocky feet. Broad subtle planar shading, 6-10-sided cylinders, bevelled cubes and simple low-segment rounded volumes. Matte flat color materials, no painted texture detail. Approximately 500-1000 triangle modeling intention per character, not an asserted mesh count. Gentle soft light, very subtle contact shadow, clear detached limbs and weapon silhouettes. Plush visual proportions but solid simple mesh geometry. Eyes just two small dark dots, little noses, animal snouts simple wedges.
MOUSE BUILDER: warm tan mouse with two huge round disc ears, small wedge snout, sage short hoodless tunic, blue shoulder patch as team color, one oversized wooden construction mallet held away from body, tiny square tool bag. Tail short thick curved taper, visible but not essential to recognition. NO fingers, complex rope, pickaxe or axe. Key overhead shapes: round ears and block mallet.
HEDGEHOG GUARD: short wide brown hedgehog with tan face and one broad rounded dark-brown back shell representing quills using ONLY five or six large geometric lobes, no individual needles. Blue padded tabard. Huge rounded-square wooden shield on left and one short leaf-shaped sword on right. Ears small, muzzle short. Key overhead shape: quill shell plus large shield.
SQUIRREL CROSSBOW: rust-orange squirrel, cream muzzle, small rounded triangular ears, teal vest with blue shoulder patch, oversized simple T-shaped crossbow held forward in both mitten paws. One broad thick curled tail rises behind the body like a simple comma made of 3 chunky connected volumes; keep tail distinct from weapon and avoid complexity. Key overhead shapes: transverse crossbow and chunky tail.
BADGER WARDEN: stocky grey badger with cream-and-charcoal facial stripe material regions, broad blunt snout, short round ears, moss tunic, large blue shoulder mantle formed from one thick flat shape, simple acorn-shaped bronze chest clasp, broad short wooden hafted hammer at right and round wood shield at left. Body wider than the other units; no elaborate armour. Key overhead shapes: broad shoulders, striped head and round shield.
Use team blue consistently across every unit while species and natural material colors remain different. Show construction-friendly designs, no delicate cloth folds, fur strands, needle quills, individual toes or fingers, ornate weapons, tiny buckles, realistic materials, busy scenery or ground bases. Strong readable shape at 48 pixels high. This is a cohesive charming approachable army of woodland people, not realistic animals and not highly detailed collectible miniatures.
